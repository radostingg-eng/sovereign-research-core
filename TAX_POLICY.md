# Czech tax-aware investment policy

This file is a planning-control document, not tax advice.

## Current verified baseline

For 2026, the Czech Financial Administration states that the CZK 40 million ceiling for exemption of individuals' income from sales of securities and business interests was removed from 1 January 2026 when the relevant time test is met. It also states that the CZK 100,000 value test remains in place for securities, and that the holding-period test remains a separate route to exemption.

The system therefore treats the user's preference to hold investments for at least three years when practical as a planning preference aligned with the current securities time-test framework, not as a blanket tax rule for every instrument or transaction.

## Operating rule

Before a material realization decision, verify the current Czech tax treatment for the exact instrument, acquisition history, transaction type, and applicable exemption conditions from an authoritative Czech source. Do not infer tax treatment from the age of a position alone.

Source checked 2026-09-15: Czech Financial Administration, “Daňové novinky pro rok 2026” and its individual-income-tax guidance.
